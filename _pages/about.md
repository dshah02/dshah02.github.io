Hi! I am Devan Shah, a recent graduate of Princeton, where I am very grateful to have been advised by Prof. <a href="https://www.ehazan.com">Elad Hazan</a>. I am broadly interested in information flow within LLMs, accelerating inference of State Space Models, and LLMs for mathematics.

Some notable things worth mentioning:
<ul>
  <li>Thesis in convex training & fast inference of SSMs (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17868">SpectraLDS</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03766">FutureFill</a>).</li>
  <li>Excited by the problems and promise of LLM super-human teaching.</li>
  <li>Recipient of the <a href="https://www.cs.princeton.edu/news/class-day-department-celebrates-graduates-0">Phillip Goldman '86 Senior Prize </a> (Princeton's top CS honor) and the <a href="https://www.cs.princeton.edu/news/class-day-department-celebrates-graduates-0">Lore von Jaskowsky Memorial Prize</a> (Princeton SEAS's top research honor).</li>
</ul>

I've had the pleasure of interning and researching at <a href="https://deepmind.google">Google DeepMind</a>, <a href="https://www.janestreet.com">Jane Street</a>, and <a href="https://www.tiktok.com">TikTok/ByteDance</a>. Outside research, I've been honored to lead <a href="https://princeton.acm.org">Princeton ACM</a>.
